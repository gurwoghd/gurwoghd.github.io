## Overview

The online Jordan track of the WFK IT Volunteer Program, which I joined in the same period as the on-site [Laos track]({{ '/activity/wfk-it-volunteer-laos/' | relative_url }}). Unlike the Laos track, it had no cultural exchange part. It was teaching only.

The learners were university students in Jordan. We set up a class in Google Classroom, posted the lesson materials there, and taught live over Google Meet.

## My Role

I was in a team of three. We took turns teaching, one person per lesson.

## What I Did

**ICT lessons**

- Python basics.
- Data handling and plotting with NumPy, pandas, and Matplotlib.
- Machine learning basics with scikit-learn: linear regression, support vector machines (SVM), and the perceptron.
- The course also covered image segmentation and fine-tuning. A teammate who knew these topics well taught them, and I assisted.

**Tools:** Python, NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, scikit-learn, Google Classroom, Google Meet

## Challenges

**Topics outside what I knew.** I did not know image segmentation or fine-tuning well enough to teach them, so I asked a teammate who did to take those lessons. In return, I asked what materials the lessons needed and learned enough from that teammate to act as an assistant during class.

**Running two tracks at once.** I was teaching in Laos at the same time, so preparation time was tight. The two tracks shared much of their content, but the Laos learners had less English and less programming background and needed more time to follow. With my ICT teammate in Laos, I separated what both tracks could share from what had to be different or reinforced for Laos, and we split the preparation between us on that basis.

## What I Learned

- **Deciding what matters makes time management possible.** Doing both tracks at once often left me short of time. I found that if I kept clear what had to be done, what did not, and how to divide the necessary work, I could not only keep up with the schedule but also improve the quality of the lessons.
